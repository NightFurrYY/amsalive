TRANSPARENCY & SAFETY

What AMSA does — and doesn't do

How the assessment works

Amy guides you through three introductory prompts, a 10-item self-report questionnaire, and optional camera/microphone collection. The questionnaire is scored by fixed rules on the server. If experimental AI models are available, they provide separate descriptive classifications; otherwise the report says unavailable.

Scientific limitations

AMSA is a research prototype, not a diagnostic device. Facial expressions and voice features cannot reliably reveal a person's mental-health condition, truthfulness, or suicide risk. AI classifications are not combined with the questionnaire score. Youth questionnaire cutoffs have not been validated here. Questionnaire wording and licensing must be reviewed before public clinical use.

Privacy

The server does not store your assessment history or recordings. Optional images and audio are transmitted for analysis only after your consent. Your saved dashboard stays in this browser's local storage, which is not suitable for sensitive information on shared devices. Report PDFs are generated in memory.

Safety

AMSA does not replace a mental-health professional. If you may hurt yourself or someone else, seek immediate human support and contact local emergency services. The chatbot does not provide emergency monitoring.
